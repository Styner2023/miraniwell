## 1. What Is Mirani Well?

Mirani Well is a mental health and wellness platform — think **"Duolingo for emotional healing."** Users complete daily micro-rituals, self-assessments, affirmations, and gentle check-ins that build resilience over time.

The platform spans a **mobile app** and **VR immersive experiences**, grounded in elemental wisdom (Earth, Water, Fire, Air). It is designed specifically for **healthcare workers** seeking daily emotional balance, resilience, and reflective healing between or after work shifts.

**Founder:** Dr. Uzo Nwankpa

---

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone

All copy must follow these guidelines from Dr. Uzo:

- **Warm, gentle, non-directive** — never clinical, never pushy
- **Invitational language** — "What do you feel rising in you today?" NOT "Rate your mood 1-10"
- **Strength-based and restorative** — users are healing, not broken
- **No direct mention of burnout, trauma, or diagnosis** — use "emotional balance," "resilience," "reflective healing," "restoration"
- **Reflective and rhythmic pacing** — especially in ritual/feature descriptions
- **Encourage emotion without demanding it** — soft invitations, not instructions
- **Avoid clinical/medical jargon** — focus on embodiment and presence
- **Culturally sensitive** — rooted in elemental wisdom traditions, treated with respect

### Affirmation Samples (use as tone references):
- **Earth:** "You are rooted in purpose."
- **Water:** "Let it flow through you. You are allowed to release."
- **Fire:** "Your spark is sacred. Let it glow again."
- **Air:** "You are light enough to rise again."
- **General:** "You do not carry it alone. You are remembered. You are restoring."

## 3. The Four Elements Framework

Users are assigned an elemental healing path based on a visual/mood self-assessment. Each element has its own color palette, affirmations, rituals, and visual metaphors:

| Element | Colors | Visual Metaphors | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Earth** | Deep greens, browns, moss textures | Trees, roots, soil, grounding stones | Stability, grounding, purpose |
| **Water** | Flowing blues, glowing turquoise, fluid light | Rivers, rain, ocean, flowing streams | Release, flow, emotional processing |
| **Fire** | Warm reds, ambers, golden glows | Flames, candles, embers, sunrise | Passion, renewal, inner spark |
| **Air** | Light sky tones, white mist, soft gradients | Clouds, breath, open sky, wind | Lightness, clarity, rising above |

## 4. Product Features — What Mirani Well Does

### Mobile App (The "Duolingo" Experience):
- **Profile Setup** — Name, language, accessibility options
- **Wellbeing Index Survey** — 8–10 reflective questions (initial baseline + 30-day follow-up)
- **Elemental Self-Assessment** — Visual/mood-based questions that assign users to a healing path (Earth, Water, Fire, or Air)
- **Daily Micro-Rituals** — 2–5 minute practices: breathwork, visualization, journaling, sound/toning
- **Elemental Affirmations** — Push-delivered or on-demand, matched to the user's element
- **Progress Dashboard** — Rituals completed, affirmations collected, mood trends
- **Soft Invitations to VR** — Based on engagement or time since last ritual

### VR Immersive Experience:
- Elemental Ritual Rooms (Water or Earth environment for MVP)
- Immersive nature-based environments
- Guided audio rituals
- Post-ritual affirmation system
- Healing progress visuals (tree, water, fire, or cloud that grows with use)

### The Gamification Layer (Duolingo Parallel):
- Daily streaks and gentle nudges (not punitive — never guilt-based)
- Element-themed progress paths (like skill trees)
- Unlockable deeper rituals as users engage
- Encouraging "return" prompts
- Visual progress that feels like growth (growing tree, flowing river, rising flame, clearing sky)

### Core Expressive Arts Ritual Elements:
- **Breathwork:** Guided inhales/exhales with nature sounds
- **Gentle movement:** Shoulder rolls, stretches, sways
- **Sound:** Toning, humming, voice-led affirmations
- **Visualization:** Guided imagery tied to user's element
- **Journaling:** Optional reflective prompts (never forced)

## 6. Target Audience

**Primary:** Healthcare workers (nurses, doctors, therapists, caregivers) seeking emotional balance between or after shifts

**Secondary:** General public interested in daily wellness practices, mindfulness, and gentle emotional tracking

**Tertiary (Future):** Enterprise/organizational wellness programs (anonymous dashboards)

## 7. Technical Platform Summary (For Accurate Copy)

- **Mobile:** iOS + Android (Flutter or React Native, cross-platform)
- **VR:** Meta Quest 2/3 (Unity)
- **Backend:** Firebase or equivalent HIPAA-compliant architecture
- **Privacy:** All data encrypted; emotional responses stored privately; aggregate summaries only with explicit opt-in
- **Authentication:** Email or secure mobile login (no social login required)
- **Accessibility:** Screen reader compatible, adjustable font/contrast/sound, closed captions for VR, seated VR option
- **HIPAA Compliant** — this is a key trust signal for the website

### Future Expansion Phases:
- **Phase 2:** Sensor integration (HRV, steps, sleep patterns for burnout risk)
- **Phase 3:** Group Elemental Circles and live/recorded healing sessions
- **Enterprise:** Organizational dashboards (anonymous only)
- **AI:** Language support and multi-region access
